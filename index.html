October 2 • For Dii
💌

Happy Birthday, Dii. 🎂❤️

Today is October 2, your day. I've been thinking for days about how to say what I feel, and every time I sat down to write, I realised there are not enough words for it. So I'll do the only thing I know how to do: I'll be real with you, the way you've always let me be.

Do you remember 16 February? Two strangers, no plans, no idea what was coming. If someone had told me that day that you would become the person I can't imagine my life without, I would have laughed. And yet here we are. I still don't know how it happened, or when, or why. I just know that somewhere between that first day and today, you stopped being a stranger and became my Dii.

You are not my sister by blood, Dii. You are my sister by heart, and I honestly think that is the stronger kind. Blood is something you are born with. This bond is something we chose, quietly and without a single promise, and that is why it feels so real to me.

There is so much I want to thank you for.

Thank you for being one of the sweetest and kindest people I have ever met.

Thank you for understanding me without words. With you, I never feel like I have to explain myself. Somehow you just know, and that feeling is rarer than you think.

Thank you for the days when life felt heavy and you reminded me that I'm stronger than I believe. Sometimes I forget that, and you never do.

Thank you for letting me be imperfect. Around you, I don't have to pretend or perform. I can just be real, and somehow that is always enough for you. Do you know how rare that gift is?

And thank you for that one moment I will never forget. Once, when it felt like someone didn't accept me, you still spoke up and asked why they didn't like your brother. It was a small sentence for you, but for me it meant the world. It showed me that you had truly accepted me as your brother. I think that was the moment I knew this bond was real.

🌙 13 April — The Day You Chose to Stay

There is one date forever stamped on my heart: April 13. That was the day you wanted to walk away, and the day we spoke on a call for the very first time. I still remember the weight of every word, the way neither of us could hold back the tears, and the ache in my chest fearing I was going to lose you. But through all the heartache and tears, you didn't leave me. You chose to stay. That will forever be the day you proved our bond was real, and I will remember it with gratitude for the rest of my life.

📞 Our Daily Streak — An Unbroken Promise

And then we built something so rare: an unbroken streak of months upon months where not a single day went by without hearing each other's voice. No matter how busy or exhausting life got, or whatever happened in our days, we made sure to talk on that call. Having you there every single day became my greatest comfort, my favorite routine, and one of the best things that has ever happened to me.

🎮 Gaming Together — Our Little Love Language

Playing games with you and spending hours together has become our own special love language. I know I’m still such a noob at the game, but the way you always look out for me melts my heart. You patiently correct me, guide me through every part, and make the whole game easier by handing over all your own items and gear just so I don't struggle. Everyone knows you love me so much, Dii... but the truth is, I love you even more (hehehe).

Somewhere along the way, we became the kind of people who can't stay without each other. That is a beautiful thing to be able to say about someone, and I'm grateful for it every single day.

On your birthday, I want to make you some promises. I can't fix all your problems, and I won't always have the right answer. But you will never have to face anything alone. No matter what happens, I will always have time for you. And if you are ever going through something difficult, Khuda na khasta, please never hesitate to share it with this brother. I'll listen first and worry about solutions later.

I hope this year is kinder to you than the last. I hope every wish you make today finds its way to you. I hope you get all the love you give to everyone around you, and that you laugh so much your face hurts.

I also want to ask you for something, Dii. Please stay in my life, no matter what happens in the future. Your presence makes me feel safe and happy, and I honestly don't want to imagine how it would feel to lose you. And please, kabhi chhod ke mat jaana.

You are my lifeline, my Dii, my family by choice. I love you more than these words can carry.

Happy Birthday, Dii. Always yours,

Your brother,
Kabeer ❤️
